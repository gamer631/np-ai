# NIL.ai — Nepal Innovation Labs

**Brand:** NIL.ai  
**Legal/company name:** Nepal Innovation Labs (proposed; subject to registration and clearance)  
**Positioning:** Nepal-born AI research and product engineering company  
**Tagline:** Born in Nepal. Built for the world.  
**Design direction:** Advanced research laboratory — precise, calm, technical, accessible.

## Mission

Research, engineer and deliver original, useful, reliable AI technology for people and organizations in Nepal and worldwide.

## Principles

1. Evidence before claims: publish reproducible evaluations and disclose limitations.
2. Useful innovation: build around validated customer and community problems.
3. Responsible AI: protect privacy, secure data, prevent unauthorized actions, and provide human oversight.
4. Open science where appropriate: publish benchmarks, methods and non-sensitive research artifacts under clear licences.
5. Sustainable business: earn revenue by delivering measurable value; never monetize customer data without explicit, informed authorization.
6. Nepal-rooted, globally usable: support Nepali and English while designing for international accessibility.

## Product and research portfolio

- **NP.ai (flagship product):** a multilingual AI workspace for grounded document understanding and approved workflows.
- **NIL Research:** evaluation, Nepali language technology, retrieval reliability, efficient inference and AI safety.
- **NIL Platform:** reusable model-provider adapters, APIs and developer infrastructure.
- **NIL Labs (future):** applied experiments in robotics, edge AI and intelligent hardware. This is exploratory, not an active delivery commitment.

## NP.ai first product boundary

The first customer problem is: help a small organization ask questions about its own documents and receive useful answers with traceable source passages.

Initial scope:
- Organization-scoped workspaces.
- Private document upload and extraction for explicitly supported formats.
- Permission-filtered retrieval.
- Answers with document and page/section references where available.
- Clear abstention when evidence is missing.
- Usage, cost and audit visibility for administrators.

Out of scope until separately designed and validated:
- Autonomous financial or legal actions.
- Training a foundation model.
- Unrestricted agent tool execution.
- Public sharing of private documents.
- Claims of guaranteed correctness.

## Company website

The public NIL.ai website is the company and research front door, not the NP.ai application. It should explain the mission, products, research, responsible-AI commitments, and contact path. Keep marketing pages separate from authenticated product routes.

## Brand system

- Primary palette: deep navy / graphite, cool white, restrained cyan or electric blue accent.
- Visual language: laboratory grids, precise diagrams, subtle motion, real product screenshots and research artefacts.
- Avoid: generic AI stock imagery, excessive gradients, fake metrics, fabricated partners, fake testimonials and unsupported claims.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA target, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, semantic landmarks, readable contrast and responsive layouts.

## Trust and governance

- Never expose provider keys in browser code or repository history.
- Enforce tenant isolation in the database and every retrieval path.
- Treat uploaded content as untrusted data; it cannot override system policy or permissions.
- Use explicit consent and retention rules for personal data.
- Provide deletion/export workflows where applicable.
- Maintain security reviews, incident response notes, backups and dependency updates.
- Publish research datasets only with documented provenance, consent and licensing.

## Measures of progress

Product:
- Successful task completion rate.
- Grounded answer accuracy and citation support.
- Unsupported-answer / abstention quality.
- Median and p95 latency.
- Cost per successful task.
- Retention and paid conversion.

Research:
- Dataset provenance and licence coverage.
- Inter-annotator agreement.
- Benchmark reproducibility.
- Results across standard Nepali, Romanized Nepali and code-switching.
- Published limitations and error analysis.

Business:
- Customer interviews and validated pain points.
- Active organizations.
- Paid renewals.
- Gross margin after model and infrastructure costs.
- Customer-reported time saved.
