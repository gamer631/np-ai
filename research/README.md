# NIL Research

NIL Research is the research and evaluation program of Nepal Innovation Labs. This directory is for reproducible methods, dataset documentation, evaluation protocols and published results.

## Research integrity

- Separate hypotheses, planned experiments, measured results and conclusions.
- Never publish results without a versioned dataset, evaluation code, model/provider identifiers, configuration and date.
- Record dataset provenance, consent, licence, allowed uses and removal requests.
- Do not place private customer documents, credentials, personal data or unlicensed material in research datasets.
- Keep a documented train/dev/test separation where applicable; prevent test-set leakage.
- Publish limitations, known failure modes and uncertainty alongside headline metrics.
- Do not describe third-party model API access as a proprietary foundation model.

## Initial program

The first proposed study is a Nepali-English language and grounded-document benchmark. Its protocol is in [Nepali AI Benchmark v0.1](./nepali-ai-benchmark-v0.1.md).

## Status vocabulary

- **Proposed:** design only; no data collection or experiment completed.
- **In progress:** data or evaluation work has begun; report exact scope.
- **Reproduced:** another run reproduces a reported result under the documented setup.
- **Published:** methods, versioned artifacts, limitations and results are available under an explicit licence.

Nothing in this directory should be interpreted as a completed benchmark until the results and artifacts are actually produced.
